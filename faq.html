FAQs

The questions you answer on the phone every week.

Every one of these belongs here instead, so you stop repeating yourself.

How much is delivery?

Delivery starts at $35 for the local area &mdash; that is your own published figure from your Yelp answers. DRAFT: confirm how far that rate reaches and what it becomes further out.

Do you deliver and set up?

DRAFT - confirm. For tents, dance floors and pipe and drape the answer matters more than anything else on this page, because nobody wants to lay a dance floor themselves.

How far ahead should I book?

DRAFT - your real answer. Wedding and quinceañera season fills up, and saying so sets expectations before somebody calls you the week before.

Can you set up on grass or concrete?

DRAFT - confirm, especially for tents and dance floors. Anchoring differs and customers genuinely do not know whether their yard rules them out.

What is your deposit and cancellation policy?

DRAFT - decide and publish it. It is the question that causes every dispute, and having it written down beforehand is how you avoid them.

Are you insured?

DRAFT - important for tents and inflatables. Many venues will not let equipment on site without a certificate of insurance naming them. If you can issue one, say so here.

Can I call or text?

Both. DRAFT - confirm which number you prefer for each; you have given out a separate text line in your Yelp answers and it should be on the site properly rather than buried in a Q&amp;A.

Tell us the date and how many people.

We will put the list together and quote it with delivery, setup and pickup included.
